Help

Thanks for using this web app! Here's how to get started with collecting event data:

Quickstart
  1. Select a grip, shot type, and outcome from the menu using the mouse or keyboard shortcuts in brackets.
  2. Select a player from the player menu via the mouse or keyboard shortcuts in brackets.
  3. Click the area of the court where the action occured.
  4. To record the start and end points of an action, click and drag the mouse across the court.
  5. Press Enter to add an event without coordinates.
  6. Press Backspace to remove the last event.
Customize Player Details

Click on the "Edit" button next to the Home Player or Away Player heading to open a pop-up. Add additional information (jersey numbers, player names) to customize the exported CSV and PDF files.

Generate CSV and PDF Reports

After tagging the events, click on the "Download CSV" or "Download Report" button to obtain a CSV file of the table or a PDF report that details the actions taken by each player.
