Sports Event Tracker

Event tagging made simple and accessible.

Click on a sport to start tracking actions on the pitch or court, then export your data as CSV or generate a PDF report.

Quickstart
  1. Pick a sport from the list below.
  2. Select player and action from the right-side controls using your mouse or keyboard shortcuts.
  3. Click or drag between points on the court to record start and end coordinates.
  4. Customize event and player details using the Edit buttons.
  5. Export data via the Download CSV or Download Report buttons.
